# The Quiet Gambit: UX and accessibility review of the build

Scope: index.html, css/styles.css, js/board.js, js/app.js, plus DOM checks on http://localhost:5173 at 320×640, 375×667, 768×1024, 900, and 1280×800. Contrast figures are WCAG 2.x values computed from the CSS colours actually used, including the `color-mix` results. Forced-colors was reviewed from the code only; I could not emulate it.

**What works:** the first screen answers what, where and why. The H1, the lede ("12 short lessons… play the move… see why"), a single ink primary CTA driven by progress, and a secondary "See the learning path" all sit above the fold at 320×640 (CTA at y≈473). The hero puzzle comes after the copy on mobile. The board model is solid. There is one tab stop per board. Arrows move visually, including when the board is flipped. Enter/Space selects, moves or deselects. Esc cancels. Focus stays on the destination square after a move. Labels read like "e7, empty, legal move". Each board has its own polite live region, and feedback has a separate `role=status`. Each board has a "Position in words" panel. Every route sets the title, focuses the h1 and sets `aria-current`. The 320px menu works: links are 52px tall and Esc returns focus to the button. The "Next step" button uses `aria-disabled` plus a visible reason. The reset confirm moves focus to Cancel. Empty and complete states exist. Using focusable `role=gridcell` divs instead of buttons inside gridcells is acceptable: the APG grid pattern allows focusable cells, `aria-label` on a gridcell is announced by NVDA, JAWS and VoiceOver, and activation is handled by keydown plus a `detail===0` click for AT. Keep it, but see P3-1.

## P1: must fix
1. **The skip link breaks the page.** `index.html:17` uses `href="#main"`. That fires `hashchange`, and the router (`app.js:860-862`) treats `main` as a route, so it renders "That page is not on the board". Verified: `location.hash` becomes `#main` and the h1 becomes Not found. Fix: in the router, ignore hashes that don't start with `#/`, and add `skip.addEventListener('click', e => { e.preventDefault(); main.focus(); main.scrollIntoView(); })`.
2. **The header overflows from 761px to about 900px.** The compact-nav band (`styles.css:143-146`) still shows the wordmark, 6 links and the progress chip (`index.html:37`). At 768 the chip ends at x=880 in a 753px viewport, so the page scrolls sideways. "Learning path" also wraps onto two lines (58px). At 900 the chip touches the edge. Fix: move the menu breakpoint to `max-width: 960px` (both `760px` queries, `styles.css:147`), or hide `.progress-chip` below 1000px and add `white-space: nowrap` to `.nav a`.

## P2: should fix
3. **On phones, feedback and the prompt are off-screen during a puzzle.** At 375×667, after the step scroll, the board spans y 99–424. The prompt starts at 658, the action buttons sit at 778–862, and the feedback renders below them, out of view. The result of a move is invisible, and under reduced motion the red flash is gone too. Fix: below 900px, render the `.feedback` node directly after the board frame, inside `#lesson-board` (`app.js:260-268`, `774-776`). Also put the prompt above the board, or make the lesson head (`lede`) collapsible on mobile.
4. **Focus is lost after "Show answer" and after reset.** `finish()` sets `disabled` on Hint and Show answer (`app.js:298-299`), so the focused button disappears and focus falls to `<body>` (verified). The reset "yes" path calls `render()` (`app.js:675-678`) and loses focus the same way. Fix: after solve or reveal, focus the "Next step" or "Finish lesson" button (renderNav is already called in `onSolved`, `app.js:781`). After reset, focus the page h1.
5. **Tap targets are under 44px.** Measured: Menu 63×36 (`index.html:25`, `.btn-sm` `styles.css:180`); Hint, Show answer and Reset position all 36px tall (`app.js:263-265`); step-track buttons 43×35 (`styles.css:489-493`); stepper buttons 44×36; move-list buttons 30×31 (`styles.css:411-413`); explorer chips 42px tall. Fix: `@media (pointer: coarse) { .btn-sm, .step-track button { min-height: 44px } .movelist button { min-height: 44px; padding: 8px } }`, or simply give `.btn-sm` `min-height: 44px` everywhere.
6. **Coordinates are too small.** `font-size: calc(100cqw / 8 * 0.17)` (`styles.css:310`) gives 5.7px at 320, 6.9px at 375 and 8–11px on desktop. Fix: `font-size: max(11px, calc(100cqw / 8 * 0.2))`, or move the coordinates into the paper frame outside the squares.
7. **The hint marker fails contrast on dark squares.** `.is-highlight` uses `accent` at 70% (`styles.css:321`), which is 2.23:1 on dark squares and 3.48 on light. That marker is the whole hint ("the piece to move is marked"). Fix: a solid `var(--accent-board)` ring (4.02 on dark), plus a shape cue such as corner brackets.
8. **The focus ring is invisible on the ink "next card".** The ultramarine outline on `#1A1D2B` is 2.17:1 on `/path` (`styles.css:460-464`). Fix: `.next-card :focus-visible { outline-color: var(--paper); }`.
9. **Step-track buttons have no visible boundary.** The border is `var(--rule)`, 1.35:1 (`styles.css:491`). The current step is shown only by an ink border. Fix: `border-color: var(--control-border)`, and add a 2px or filled style for `aria-current`.
10. **Reduced motion speeds up the pacing.** `later()` caps every delay at 120ms (`app.js:38`). Under reduced motion the opponent reply and the Show-answer playback (700ms per move) run at 120ms, so moves blur together. Each `announce()` also overwrites the last one, so screen readers hear only the final move. Pauses are not motion. Fix: delete the `REDUCED` clamp in `later()` and let CSS handle animation.
11. **Moves are announced twice, and in notation.** After a correct move the board announces the reply in words ("Black pawn…") while feedback says "Ne7 is right. Black replied exd5" (`app.js:321,328`). Wrong-move titles say "Nb6 is not the move" (`app.js:314`). Fix: leave the move text out of feedback titles ("Right. Now find the follow-up"), and expose a `describeMove` from board.js for any wording that must name a move.
12. **The line stepper doesn't announce the move.** Only `.move-note` is live (`app.js:185`). `.stepper-pos` ("Move 3 of 9: 3. Bc4") isn't, and `setPosition` never announces, so a screen-reader user hears a note without the move it explains. Fix: in `show()` (`app.js:189-207`), call `frame.board.announce('Move 3, White bishop to c4. ' + note)` and drop `aria-live` from `.move-note`.
13. **The board's orientation isn't announced.** Grid labels are "Lesson board" or "X board" (`app.js:719`, `board.js:31`). Puzzles flip to Black (`app.js:287`), and then ArrowUp moves toward rank 1 without warning. Fix: on every `setOrientation`/`render`, set `aria-label = label + ', ' + (orientation === 'w' ? 'White' : 'Black') + ' at bottom'`.
14. **Forced colors makes the board unusable.** In forced-colors mode, white and black glyphs both become CanvasText and so look identical. The light/dark square backgrounds, the last-move tint, the capture ring (box-shadow), the hint ring (box-shadow) and the red check all disappear. Selection and focus become the same Highlight outline (`styles.css:550-554`). Fix: `.board-wrap { forced-color-adjust: none; }` to keep the board's own palette. Keep `.sq:focus-visible { outline: 3px solid Highlight; outline-offset: -3px }` and make selection a different style, e.g. `outline: 3px dashed CanvasText`.

## P3: nice to have
1. Write `aria-selected` only on the selected cell. Right now all 64 cells carry `aria-selected="false"` (`board.js:86`), and some screen readers announce "not selected" on every cell. On read-only boards, use `aria-readonly="true"` instead of `aria-disabled="true"` (`board.js:102`), so cells aren't announced as "unavailable".
2. When a puzzle loads, put the default roving focus on the side-to-move's king or the hinted piece instead of the bottom-left square (`board.js:71`). Add Ctrl+Home/Ctrl+End (`board.js:273-274`).
3. Tapping an empty square that isn't a legal move currently flashes red and announces "not legal" (`board.js:146-148`). Make it a silent deselect. Note that `touch-action: none` on movable squares (`styles.css:291`) stops a page scroll that starts on your own pieces. Consider applying `none` only once a drag starts.
4. A wrong move is rejected instantly, so the learner never sees it on the board. Hold it for about 600ms, then undo. Under reduced motion, replace the 0.7s `flash-bad` animation (`styles.css:336-339`, which the reduced-motion rule zeroes) with a static tint toggled by timeout.
5. Check is shown only by a red glow, 2.46:1 on dark squares (`styles.css:325`), and the king's label doesn't say "in check" (`board.js:87-91`). Append ", in check" to the label. The alternate arrow is 2.68:1 on dark squares (`styles.css:344`).
6. On desktop the lesson board ignores viewport height: at 1280×800 its bottom edge is at 802. Use `max-width: min(600px, calc(100svh - 140px))` (`styles.css:486`).
7. The pieces are Unicode glyphs with `-webkit-text-stroke` and are readable. The piece font uses `display=block` (`index.html:13`), so boards can be blank for up to 3s on slow networks. Prefer `swap`, or inline SVG pieces as recommended before.
8. The chip's accessible name "Progress: 3 of 12 lessons done…" doesn't contain its visible text "3 of 12 done" (`app.js:87`), which fails 2.5.3 label-in-name for voice-control users. Start the name with the visible text.
9. The primary CTA says "Continue with lesson 1" when lesson 1 was never opened and only another lesson is done (`app.js:402`). Use "Start lesson N" unless that lesson itself has been started.
10. The "Words you will meet" section on /path contains only a link (`app.js:659`). Remove it or fill it. The "Position in words" summary is 21px tall: pad it to 24px or more.
11. Each `B.create` adds three `window` pointer listeners that are never removed (`board.js:216,231,252`). They pile up across route changes. Scope them to the grid with `setPointerCapture`.
